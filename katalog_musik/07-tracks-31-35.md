# Batch 7 — Lagu 31–35 (Mini-Game akhir + Cutscene awal)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.

---

## 31. Mini-Game — Library Stealth
*(Mini-game menyelinap di perpustakaan tanpa ketahuan)*

- **Story/Mood:** Tegang-lucu, hati-hati — menyelinap melewati penjaga perpustakaan yang galak. "Ssst!" suspense komikal.
- **Chord Progression:** `Em – Em(maj7) – Em7 – Em6` (line cliché menurun) creeping; resolusi `Am – B7`.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Loop tip-toe 8 bar minim; build tension bertahap → release saat aman.
- **Instrumen:** Pizzicato strings, walking upright bass, muted vibraphone, brush snare, finger snap, "shh" SFX, glockenspiel aksen.
- **Referensi Genre:** Spy-jazz stealth, *Pink Panther* sneaky, lounge-noir.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Sneak** (pizzicato pelan) → **Spotted!** (drum + brass blast, tempo melonjak) saat hampir ketahuan; **clear** stinger lega.

---

## 32. Mini-Game — Motorbike Chase
*(Mini-game kejar-kejaran motor antar kampus)*

- **Story/Mood:** Cepat, adrenalin, seru — balap/kejar motor menembus jalanan kampus. Penuh kecepatan dan manuver.
- **Chord Progression:** `A5 – C5 – D5 – F5` (riff naik) high-energy; chorus `D – A – E` driving.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro engine rev → A (fast riff) → B (turbo build) → **Loop** seamless cepat.
- **Instrumen:** Fast surf/rock guitar, driving drums (16th hi-hat), synth bass, organ, engine/horn SFX, tambourine.
- **Referensi Genre:** Surf-rock chase, *OutRun* arcade, high-speed rockabilly.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Cruise** (riff dasar) → **Nitro** (tempo terasa naik + lead solo) saat boost; **near-miss** stinger saat hampir tabrakan.

---

## 33. Mini-Game — Rhythm Mabar
*(Mini-game ritme/menari bareng — "main bareng")*

- **Story/Mood:** Fun, groovy, party — tantangan tekan tombol mengikuti irama. Ceria, sosial, bikin pengen ikut gerak.
- **Chord Progression:** `Bb – Gm – Eb – F` (I–vi–IV–V) dance-pop; drop instrumental berbasis groove.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro count-in → Verse → Pre → Drop (puncak ritme) → break → **Loop**; struktur jelas untuk cue notasi ritme.
- **Instrumen:** Synth lead, sidechain pad, four-on-floor + clap, slap/synth bass, vocal chops, riser & impact SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Dance-pop / future-funk, *DDR/Project Diva* vibe, K-pop-EDM.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Easy/Normal/Hard** (kepadatan layer & fill berbeda) → **Fever/Perfect** (full stack, vocal chop + riser) saat combo tinggi.

## 34. Mini-Game — Photo Scoop (Jurnalis Quest)
*(Mini-game memotret bukti — sesuai tema UKM jurnalistik Garuda Emblem)*

- **Story/Mood:** Penasaran, cerdas, sedikit menegangkan — membidik foto bukti diam-diam untuk berita. Investigatif dan stylish.
- **Chord Progression:** `Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 – A7b13` (ii–V–I jazzy) detektif; bridge kromatis "mengintai".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Loop 16 bar groovy detektif; aksen "snap" tiap foto berhasil.
- **Instrumen:** Muted trumpet, brushed drums, walking bass, Rhodes, vibraphone, kamera shutter SFX, finger snap.
- **Referensi Genre:** Detective-jazz/noir, *Persona* investigation, cool spy-lounge.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Stakeout** (lounge santai) → **Deadline** (tempo terasa naik, drum rapat) saat timer; **scoop** stinger saat foto target tertangkap.

## 35. Opening Prologue — How It Began
*(Cutscene pembuka cerita)*

- **Story/Mood:** Tenang lalu memanas — pengantar dunia UNION yang tampak normal, perlahan menyingkap konflik. Naratif & sinematik.
- **Chord Progression:** `C – G/B – Am – F` (I–V–vi–IV) tenang; transisi menegang ke `Dm – Bb – C – A7` saat konflik muncul.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Bagian I "kampus damai" (ambient) → transisi → Bagian II "ada yang salah" (tension build) → cliffhanger (tanpa loop, mengikuti durasi cutscene).
- **Instrumen:** Piano lembut, strings, woodwind, ambient pad, low drone, subtle percussion saat tension, chiptune motif samar (foreshadow).
- **Referensi Genre:** Cinematic intro / visual-novel prologue, score film drama-kampus.
- **Variasi Loop:** Bukan loop — sediakan **stem ambient** & **stem tension** agar bisa dipotong/disambung mengikuti panjang dialog.
