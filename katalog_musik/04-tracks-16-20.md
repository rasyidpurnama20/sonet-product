# Batch 4 — Lagu 16–20 (Stage Fakultas akhir + Boss awal)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.

---

## 16. Fakultas Olahraga — Stadium Showdown
*(Stage: area fakultas olahraga/stadion)*

- **Story/Mood:** Sporty, kompetitif, penuh hype — stadion & lapangan, sorak penonton, tantangan fisik. Energi tinggi seperti pertandingan final.
- **Chord Progression:** `A – E – F#m – D` (I–V–vi–IV) anthemic; chorus `D – E – A` clap-along.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro stadium chant → A (drive) → B (build) → Hook (anthem) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Big-room drums, stadium claps/stomps, brass, power-chord guitar, crowd chant "oi-oi", whistle SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Sports-rock anthem, *Punch-Out!!* hype, big-beat arena.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Warm-up** (groove + clap) → **Final Round** (tempo terasa naik, brass + chant penuh saat HP boss/wave terakhir).

---

## 17. Fakultas Pertanian — Greenhouse Rumble
*(Stage: area fakultas pertanian/rumah kaca)*

- **Story/Mood:** Organik, hangat, sedikit liar — rumah kaca & kebun kampus, tanaman raksasa, suasana hijau yang berubah jadi arena.
- **Chord Progression:** `G – Em – C – D` (I–vi–IV–V) folksy; bridge modal `Am – C – G` agar "berakar".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro suara alam (SFX burung/air) → A (folk groove) → B (hoedown stomp) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Banjo/mandolin, fiddle, acoustic guitar, foot-stomp percussion, jug bass, hand-clap.
- **Referensi Genre:** Folk/bluegrass-rock, *Stardew Valley* upbeat, country-stomp.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Tani** (santai, banjo) → **Rumble** (drum kit masuk, fiddle ngebut) saat combat; stinger "panen" saat wave clear.

---

## 18. Campus Plaza — Open-Air Clash
*(Stage: plaza/alun-alun kampus, hub antar fakultas)*

- **Story/Mood:** Terbuka, ramai, netral-tegang — pusat kampus tempat berbagai geng bentrok. Titik temu semua fakultas; identik & ikonik.
- **Chord Progression:** `C – G – Am – Em – F – C – Dm – G` (loop progresi panjang I–V–vi–iii–IV–I–ii–V) megah.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro fanfare ringan → A → B → C → **Loop** panjang (16–32 bar) agar tidak cepat repetitif di area besar.
- **Instrumen:** Orkestra ringan + band: strings, brass, drums, guitar, chiptune motif main theme sebagai callback.
- **Referensi Genre:** Hub/town theme epik, *Splatoon* plaza, orchestral-rock arcade.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Damai** (acoustic + strings) ↔ **Bentrok** (full band) tergantung status area; menyelipkan motif main theme (callback identitas).

---

## 19. Mid-Boss — Senior Hazer
*(Tema mid-boss: senior perundung/penjaga)*

- **Story/Mood:** Mengintimidasi tapi masih komikal — senior arogan penjaga gerbang fakultas. Sombong, kuat, tapi "bisa dikalahkan adik kelas".
- **Chord Progression:** `Em – C – D – B7` (i–VI–VII–V, harmonic minor) menekan; riff cepat di antara akord.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro taunt (SFX tawa) → A (riff) → B (tension build) → **Loop**; ada section "phase shift" 4 bar.
- **Instrumen:** Distorted guitar riff, aggressive drums, synth brass stab, bass driving, orchestral hit aksen.
- **Referensi Genre:** Mid-boss rock, *Streets of Rage* sub-boss, hard-rock arcade.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Phase 1** (riff dasar) → **Phase 2** (tempo terasa naik + lead, saat HP < 50%); **stun stinger** saat boss tumbang sementara.

---

## 20. Boss Teknik — "The Foreman"
*(Boss fakultas teknik: mandor mesin)*

- **Story/Mood:** Berat, mekanis, megah — bos bertubuh besar dengan alat berat & exoskeleton. Pertarungan industrial penuh dentuman.
- **Chord Progression:** `Dm5 – Bb5 – C5 – A5` (i–VI–VII–V power chords) drop-tuned; breakdown half-time `Dm – Gm`.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro machine roar → A (groove metal) → Breakdown (half-time) → B (double-time climax) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Heavy guitar (drop tuning), double-kick + industrial perc, orchestral brass, choir "hah!", sub-bass, hydraulic SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Industrial/groove metal boss, *Doom* + orchestral hybrid, *Metal Gear Rising*.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Phase 1** groove → **Breakdown** (telegraf serangan besar) → **Phase 2/Rage** (double-time, choir penuh) saat HP rendah.
