# Batch 2 — Lagu 06–10 (Core/UI + Stage Fakultas awal)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.

---

## 06. Scoop Secured — Victory Theme
*(Stage/level clear)*

- **Story/Mood:** Triumfan, lega, jenaka — kemenangan ala "berita besar berhasil diliput". Pendek, langsung memuaskan.
- **Chord Progression:** `C – G – F – C` (I–V–IV–I) dengan akhir plagal `F – C`; fanfare mayor cerah.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Stinger 6–10 detik: hit pembuka → rising run → resolusi → tail (tidak loop).
- **Instrumen:** Brass section, timpani roll, chiptune arpeggio, cymbal crash, glockenspiel.
- **Referensi Genre:** Victory fanfare *Streets of Rage*, jingle berita, marching brass.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Short** (clear biasa), **Grand** (clear fakultas/boss, tambah choir "hey!"), **Perfect/No-hit** (extra arpeggio + chime).

## 07. Deadline Missed — Game Over
*(Layar kalah)*

- **Story/Mood:** Kecewa tapi tetap komikal — "berita batal terbit". Sedikit melodramatis, mengajak coba lagi.
- **Chord Progression:** `Am – Dm – E7 – Am` (i–iv–V7–i) menurun, lalu deceptive `E7 – F` menggantung.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro turun → frasa sedih singkat → ending menggantung (loop pelan saat menu retry).
- **Instrumen:** Piano minor, muted trumpet "wah-wah" komikal, cello, music-box tail.
- **Referensi Genre:** Game over retro, sad-trombone gag, *Cuphead* loss screen.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Komikal** (muted trumpet menonjol) vs **Serius** (string lebih dalam saat kalah dari boss).

## 08. Print the Legend — Credits / Staff Roll
*(Daftar kredit)*

- **Story/Mood:** Reflektif, bangga, hangat. Perjalanan usai; nostalgia perjuangan Garuda Emblem sambil scroll nama tim.
- **Chord Progression:** `F – C/E – Dm – Bb – C` (IV–I–vi–IV–V), uplifting ballad; modulasi naik 1 step di reprise.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro → Verse → Chorus → Verse 2 → Chorus (modulasi) → Outro panjang (loop aman bila kredit lama).
- **Instrumen:** Piano, string ensemble, clean guitar arpeggio, soft drums, vokal "lah-lah" opsional, chiptune motif callback.
- **Referensi Genre:** Ending ballad JRPG, *Scott Pilgrim* outro, power-ballad pop-rock.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Instrumental** vs **Versi vokal**; **medley** menyelipkan motif main theme & tema fakultas.

## 09. Fakultas Teknik — Workshop Warzone
*(Stage: area teknik/bengkel)*

- **Story/Mood:** Industrial, berat, mekanis. Bengkel & lab mesin yang dikuasai preman; berisik, panas, penuh mesin & percikan api.
- **Chord Progression:** Riff power-chord `E5 – G5 – A5 – B5` (drop-tuned), tanpa banyak akord — fokus groove & palm-mute.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro mesin (SFX) → Riff A → Riff B (breakdown) → **Loop**; titik loop seamless.
- **Instrumen:** Heavy distorted guitar (drop D), double-kick drums, industrial percussion (anvil/metal hits), bass gain tinggi, synth drone.
- **Referensi Genre:** Industrial metal, *Doom (2016)* ringan, nu-metal arcade.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Patrol** (groove medium) → **Combat** (double-kick penuh + lead) → **Surge** saat wave banyak musuh.

## 10. Fakultas MIPA/Sains — Lab Chaos
*(Stage: laboratorium sains)*

- **Story/Mood:** Eksperimental, aneh, sedikit menyeramkan tapi seru — lab kimia/fisika meledak-ledak, cairan warna-warni, percobaan kabur.
- **Chord Progression:** `Dm – Bb – C – A7` (i–VI–VII–V) dengan kromatis bocor; sisipan whole-tone untuk kesan "tak stabil".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro bubbling (SFX lab) → A (groove) → B (bridge eksperimental) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Synth bleep/arpeggiator, theremin/saw lead, glitch percussion, bass elektronik, test-tube chime.
- **Referensi Genre:** Electro-funk sci-fi, *Mega Man* lab stage, synthwave eksperimental.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Stabil** (arpeggio rapi) vs **Meltdown** (tempo/pitch sedikit naik, glitch bertambah saat alarm lab).
