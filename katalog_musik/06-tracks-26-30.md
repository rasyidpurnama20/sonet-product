# Batch 6 — Lagu 26–30 (Boss Fakultas akhir + Mini-Game awal)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.

---

## 26. Boss Seni — "Maestro"
*(Boss fakultas seni: konduktor/seniman)*

- **Story/Mood:** Teatrikal, megalomania, indah-berbahaya — seniman yang menjadikan pertarungan sebagai "pertunjukan". Dramatis dan flamboyan.
- **Chord Progression:** `Am – E – Am – Dm – G – C – F – E7` (putaran i–V…–V7) gaya konser; modulasi naik di klimaks.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro tuning orkestra → A (waltz/tango groove) → B (crescendo) → Cadenza (jeda solo) → climax → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Solo violin/cello virtuoso, piano grand, full strings, harpsichord, timpani, opera vocal stab.
- **Referensi Genre:** Symphonic-theatrical boss, *Cuphead* "Grim Matchstick" vibe, baroque-metal.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Overture** (anggun, waltz) → **Finale** (tempo terasa naik, orkestra + choir penuh) saat HP rendah; *cadenza* stinger saat boss charge.

---

## 27. Boss Olahraga — "The Captain"
*(Boss fakultas olahraga: kapten tim)*

- **Story/Mood:** Kompetitif, gagah, hype maksimal — kapten atletik yang tangguh. Pertarungan terasa seperti laga final disorak penonton.
- **Chord Progression:** `E5 – B5 – C#5 – A5` (I–V–vi–IV power chords) anthemic; chorus clap-along `A – B – E`.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro whistle + crowd → A (drive) → B (build) → Anthem hook → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Power-chord guitar, big drums + stadium stomp/clap, brass, crowd chant, whistle/air-horn SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Sports-rock anthem boss, *Punch-Out!!* title bout, arena rock.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Kickoff** (anthem groove) → **Overtime** (tempo terasa naik, chant + brass penuh) saat HP rendah; clap-break saat boss stun.

---

## 28. Boss Pertanian — "Greenskeeper"
*(Boss fakultas pertanian: penjaga kebun)*

- **Story/Mood:** Liar, membumi, perkasa — penjaga raksasa dengan tanaman & alat tani. Folk yang berubah jadi brutal-megah.
- **Chord Progression:** `Em – C – G – D` (i–VI–III–VII) folk-epik; breakdown modal `Em – Am` "berakar dalam".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro angin/daun → A (folk-rock drive) → Breakdown (stomp) → B (climax) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Fiddle/violin agresif, banjo distorsi, electric guitar, tribal drums + foot-stomp, low brass, nature SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Folk-metal boss, *Stardew*-meets-metal, celtic-rock.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Harvest** (folk-rock) → **Overgrowth** (drum + fiddle full, double-time) saat HP rendah; stomp-break saat serangan besar.

## 29. Mini-Game — Collecting Trash
*(Mini-game pungut sampah di base camp — fitur kanon game)*

- **Story/Mood:** Ringan, lucu, santai-produktif — selingan bebas pukul: bersihkan area sambil kejar skor/waktu. Bikin senyum.
- **Chord Progression:** `C – Am – F – G` (I–vi–IV–V) ceria; bridge `Dm – G` playful.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Loop pendek 8–16 bar, ringan & berulang, mudah ditambah/kurangi layer sesuai progres.
- **Instrumen:** Ukulele, glockenspiel, whistle, light percussion (shaker/woodblock), tuba bass komikal, "pop/plop" SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Casual mini-game BGM, *Animal Crossing* ringan, cartoon comedy.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Normal** (santai) → **Time Crunch** (tempo terasa naik, tambah hi-hat saat waktu menipis); **clear jingle** saat target tercapai.

---

## 30. Mini-Game — Kantin Food-Stall Rush
*(Mini-game layani/masak di kantin kampus)*

- **Story/Mood:** Sibuk, kocak, penuh tekanan-menyenangkan — melayani pesanan kilat di kantin. Cepat, riang, sedikit panik.
- **Chord Progression:** `F – Dm – Bb – C` (I–vi–IV–V) bouncy; chorus `Bb – C – F` cerah.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro bell pesanan → A (bouncy groove) → B (build) → **Loop**; tempo bisa naik per "level".
- **Instrumen:** Honky-tonk piano, marimba, brass stab, swing drums, slap bass, sizzle/bell/ding SFX dapur.
- **Referensi Genre:** Cooking-game BGM (*Overcooked* vibe), ragtime-swing, cartoon jazz.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Tier 1–3** (tempo & layer bertambah tiap level) → **Last Order** (paling cepat); **combo stinger** saat pesanan beruntun benar.
