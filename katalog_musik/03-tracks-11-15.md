# Batch 3 — Lagu 11–15 (Stage Fakultas)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.

---

## 11. Fakultas Hukum — Courtyard of Order
*(Stage: area fakultas hukum)*

- **Story/Mood:** Tegas, megah, sedikit menekan — pelataran berpilar ala gedung pengadilan. Aura "otoritas" yang justru korup di dalamnya.
- **Chord Progression:** `Dm – Gm – A7 – Dm` (i–iv–V7–i) khidmat; bridge `Bb – C – F – A7` agar terasa "diadili".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro koor/orgel → A (march groove) → B (tegang) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Pipe organ/strings staccato, timpani, snare march, harpsichord, brass legato, electric bass.
- **Referensi Genre:** Neoklasik-cinematic, courtroom theme *Ace Attorney* (versi gelap), symphonic rock.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Tense** (string staccato + timpani) untuk combat; **Verdict** stinger gong saat sub-boss muncul.

---

## 12. Fakultas Kedokteran — Anatomy Alley
*(Stage: area fakultas kedokteran)*

- **Story/Mood:** Steril tapi mencekam, gelap-komikal — lorong rumah sakit kampus, bau formalin, manekin anatomi yang "hidup".
- **Chord Progression:** `Cm – Abmaj7 – Fm7 – G7b9` (i–VI–iv–V7♭9), tegang & mencekam dengan tension note.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro detak EKG (SFX) → A (ambient gelap) → B (groove naik) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Music-box dingin, synth pad gelap, pizzicato strings, heartbeat kick, glass/bell SFX, sub-bass.
- **Referensi Genre:** Horror-comedy BGM, *Silent Hill* ringan, dark synth.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Eerie** (ambient, minim ritme) → **Combat** (kick heartbeat jadi four-on-floor + lead distorsi).

---

## 13. Fakultas Ekonomi — Market Mayhem
*(Stage: area fakultas ekonomi/bisnis)*

- **Story/Mood:** Mewah, sibuk, glamor-serakah — lobi gedung bisnis, layar saham, uang beterbangan. Energi cepat dan flashy.
- **Chord Progression:** `Fmaj7 – Em7 – Dm7 – G7` (turun ii–V jazzy) groove disco-funk; chorus `Bbmaj7 – C` cerah.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro hi-hat disco → A (verse groove) → B (build) → Drop/Hook → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Funky guitar, four-on-the-floor drums, slap bass, brass stabs, electric piano, cash-register SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Disco-funk, *Jet Set Radio*, city-pop arcade.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Boom** (full disco) vs **Crash** (saat HP musuh menipis, harmoni minor "pasar jatuh").

---

## 14. Fakultas Ilmu Komputer — Server Room Riot
*(Stage: area fakultas ilkom)*

- **Story/Mood:** Digital, cepat, neon — ruang server berkabel, LED berkedip, hacker-thugs. Cyber dan adrenalin.
- **Chord Progression:** Bassline arpeggio `Em` → `C` → `G` → `D` (vi–IV–I–V) gaya synthwave; lead minor pentatonic.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro boot-up bleep → A (arpeggio drive) → B (lead solo) → **Loop** seamless.
- **Instrumen:** Synth arpeggiator, gated reverb drums, FM bass, square/saw lead, glitch & modem SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Synthwave/cyber, *Hotline Miami*, chiptune-EDM hybrid.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Idle** (arpeggio + pad) → **Combat** (drum + lead) → **Overclock** (tempo terasa naik, filter terbuka saat wave terakhir).

---

## 15. Fakultas Seni — Gallery Brawl
*(Stage: area fakultas seni/desain)*

- **Story/Mood:** Artistik, nyentrik, ekspresif — galeri & studio seni; cat berhamburan, patung jadi senjata. Penuh warna dan gaya.
- **Chord Progression:** `Am – D – Fmaj7 – E7` (i–IV–VI–V) bohemian; bridge berpindah ke mode Dorian agar "berwarna".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro brush/scratch → A (groove gypsy) → B (waltz selipan 3/4) → kembali 4/4 → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Nylon/flamenco guitar, accordion, violin, hand percussion (cajon/tambourine), upright bass, splash SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Gypsy-jazz / flamenco fusion, *Cuphead* eclectic, world-funk.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Sketch** (gitar solo, sepi) → **Masterpiece** (full ensemble) saat combo tinggi; selipan birama 3/4 untuk surprise.
