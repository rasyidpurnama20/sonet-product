# Batch 5 — Lagu 21–25 (Boss Fakultas)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.
Tema boss umumnya 2 fase: groove dasar → rage/climax saat HP rendah.

---

## 21. Boss MIPA — "Dr. Reaktor"
*(Boss fakultas sains: ilmuwan gila)*

- **Story/Mood:** Gila, elektrik, tak terduga — ilmuwan dengan zat reaktif & gadget eksperimen. Berbahaya tapi konyol.
- **Chord Progression:** `Cm – G7 – Ab – B°7` (i–V7–VI–vii°7) tegang dengan diminished; bridge whole-tone "tak stabil".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro alarm reaktor → A (electro-rock) → B (build) → Drop (climax) → **Loop**; phase-shift saat "meledak".
- **Instrumen:** Acid synth bass, distorted guitar, glitch perc, theremin lead, Tesla-coil SFX, orchestral hit.
- **Referensi Genre:** Electro-rock boss, *Mega Man* Wily stage, mad-scientist EDM.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Phase 1** (electro groove) → **Meltdown** (pitch/tempo terasa naik, glitch & lead penuh saat HP < 40%).

---

## 22. Boss Hukum — "The Prosecutor"
*(Boss fakultas hukum: jaksa penuntut)*

- **Story/Mood:** Megah, menghakimi, dramatis — duel "di ruang sidang". Setiap pukulan seperti ketukan palu hakim.
- **Chord Progression:** `Dm – A7 – Dm – Gm – A7 – Dm` (i–V7–i–iv–V7–i) khidmat; pipe-organ tegang.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro gavel hit + organ → A (march tegang) → B (objection build) → climax → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Pipe organ, full strings staccato, timpani, harpsichord lead, choir aksen, brass, gavel SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Symphonic-gothic boss, *Ace Attorney* "Objection!" intens, neoklasik metal.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Cross-examination** (string + organ) → **Final Verdict** (choir + brass penuh, tempo terasa naik) saat HP rendah.

---

## 23. Boss Kedokteran — "Scalpel"
*(Boss fakultas kedokteran: dokter bedah sinis)*

- **Story/Mood:** Dingin, presisi, mencekam — dokter bedah dengan gerak bersih dan menusuk. Horror-komikal yang elegan.
- **Chord Progression:** `Am – Fmaj7 – Dm – E7b9` (i–VI–iv–V7♭9) dengan tension; selipan kromatis "menyayat".
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro heartbeat + music-box → A (tense waltz 3/4) → B (4/4 combat) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Music-box dingin, pizzicato + sustained strings, sub-bass heartbeat, glass/metal scrape SFX, dark synth pad.
- **Referensi Genre:** Dark-waltz horror, *Bloodborne* elegan, gothic-electronic.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Operasi** (waltz 3/4 dingin) → **Code Blue** (4/4, kick + lead distorsi, EKG flatline SFX) saat HP rendah.

---

## 24. Boss Ekonomi — "The Broker"
*(Boss fakultas ekonomi: makelar serakah)*

- **Story/Mood:** Flashy, licik, glamor — bos berpakaian mewah yang "membeli" anak buah. Pertarungan stylish penuh gengsi.
- **Chord Progression:** `Fm7 – Bb7 – Ebmaj7 – Db7` (jazzy ii–V dengan tritone sub); chorus disko-minor energik.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro cash/coin SFX → A (disco-funk drive) → B (build) → Drop → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Slap bass, brass section, four-on-floor drums, electric piano, orchestral stab, coin/register SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Disco-funk boss, *Persona 5* battle vibe, big-band electro.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Bull Market** (funk penuh percaya diri) → **Bankrupt** (minor, tempo terasa naik, brass agresif) saat HP rendah.

---

## 25. Boss Ilkom — "Null Pointer"
*(Boss fakultas ilkom: hacker/AI)*

- **Story/Mood:** Digital, dingin, menakutkan-keren — bos hacker/AI yang "men-corrupt" arena. Glitch, neon, dan kode.
- **Chord Progression:** `Em – C – G – D` (vi–IV–I–V) synthwave; di "phase glitch" akord di-detune/pitch-shift sengaja.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro boot/error SFX → A (synthwave drive) → B (lead solo) → Glitch-break → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** FM synth bass, gated drums, arpeggiator, distorted digital lead, glitch/datamosh SFX, sub-bass.
- **Referensi Genre:** Cyber-synthwave boss, *Hotline Miami*, drum-and-bass hybrid.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Runtime** (synthwave stabil) → **System Crash** (drum-and-bass break, glitch & detune intens) saat HP < 30%.
