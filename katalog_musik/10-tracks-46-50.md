# Batch 10 — Lagu 46–50 (Co-op / Arcade & Bonus)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.
Batch penutup: mode arcade/co-op (sesuai fitur local multiplayer), secret stage, boss rush, dan NG+.

---

## 46. Arcade Mode — Endless Brawl
*(Mode arcade: bertahan tanpa henti, kejar skor)*

- **Story/Mood:** High-energy, adiktif, fun — wave demi wave tanpa henti. Murni keseruan arcade, bikin "satu ronde lagi".
- **Chord Progression:** `Am – G – F – G` (i–VII–VI–VII) driving; chorus `C – G – Am – F` energik.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro hype → A (drive) → B (hook) → **Loop** seamless tahan lama; layer bertambah tiap milestone skor.
- **Instrumen:** Distorted guitar, chiptune lead, fast drums, synth bass, arcade bleep, crowd "hey!" SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Arcade beat 'em up high-energy, *Streets of Rage 4* survival, J-rock + chiptune.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Wave tier** (intensitas naik tiap 5 wave) + **combo/multiplier stinger** saat skor tembus ambang; reprise motif main theme.

---

## 47. Double Trouble — Co-op Theme (Farid & Rani)
*(Tema mode co-op lokal: dua pemain)*

- **Story/Mood:** Kompak, ceria, penuh chemistry — duo Farid & Rani bertarung berdampingan. Fun, dinamis, terasa "lebih ramai".
- **Chord Progression:** `D – A – Bm – G` (I–V–vi–IV) upbeat; bridge *call & response* dua lead (Farid vs Rani) saling sahut.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro tag-team → A (groove) → B (dual-lead trade-off) → Hook → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Dua lead (guitar = Farid, synth = Rani) saling sahut, funky bass, energetic drums, brass, hand-clap.
- **Referensi Genre:** Co-op brawler theme, *Scott Pilgrim* duo, funky power-pop.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Solo** (1 lead saja, mode single) vs **Co-op** (dual-lead penuh); **team-up stinger** saat combo bareng / assist.

---

## 48. Underground Fight Club — Secret Stage
*(Stage rahasia: arena tarung bawah tanah kampus)*

- **Story/Mood:** Gelap, keren, terlarang — arena tarung ilegal di basement kampus. Underground, penuh asap, berbahaya tapi stylish.
- **Chord Progression:** `Em – Em7 – A7 – Em` (i–i7–IV7–i) bluesy-gelap; riff dorian kotor di antara akord.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro bel ring + sorak samar → A (dirty groove) → B (riff solo) → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Dirty blues-rock guitar (fuzz), organ Hammond, heavy groovy drums, walking bass tebal, harmonica aksen, chain/bell SFX.
- **Referensi Genre:** Stoner/blues-rock, underground fight vibe, dark funk-rock.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Underdog** (groove dasar, kotor) → **Knockout** (fuzz + organ penuh, tempo terasa naik) saat ronde final.

---

## 49. Boss Rush — Gauntlet
*(Mode bonus: lawan semua boss beruntun)*

- **Story/Mood:** Intens, menantang, megah — ujian pamungkas melawan semua boss tanpa jeda. Medley penuh adrenalin & gengsi.
- **Chord Progression:** Medley/modulasi — sambungkan motif boss tiap fakultas, dijahit lewat pivot chord; tonal center bergerak `Em → Am → Dm → F#m`.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro gauntlet → segmen medley (motif tiap boss, 8–16 bar) → transisi seamless antar boss → **Loop** keseluruhan.
- **Instrumen:** Orchestral-metal hybrid (gabungan palet semua boss): guitar, orchestra, choir, synth, double-kick, brass.
- **Referensi Genre:** Boss-rush medley, *Mega Man* boss-rush, symphonic-metal gauntlet.
- **Variasi Loop:** Tiap segmen punya **cue masuk/keluar** agar mulus saat boss berganti; **final stinger** saat gauntlet tuntas.

---

## 50. New Game+ — Main Theme Reprise
*(Mode NG+: aransemen ulang tema utama)*

- **Story/Mood:** Familiar tapi segar, percaya diri, "kembali lebih kuat" — penghormatan pada main theme untuk pemain veteran. Nostalgik & gagah.
- **Chord Progression:** `Am – F – C – G` (i–VI–III–VII, main theme) di-reharmonisasi: tambah `Am – Fmaj7 – Cmaj7 – G6` + key-lift di akhir.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro callback → A (tema utama versi "grown-up") → B (variasi baru) → Hook megah → **Loop**.
- **Instrumen:** Versi "dewasa" main theme: orkestra + band penuh, lead guitar virtuoso, chiptune lead nostalgia, choir, brass, timpani.
- **Referensi Genre:** NG+/credits reprise, *Sonic* final-theme reprise, epic rock-orchestral remix.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Hard-mode** (lebih cepat & garang) vs **Tribute** (lebih megah/emosional); menutup katalog dengan kembali ke identitas lagu 01.
