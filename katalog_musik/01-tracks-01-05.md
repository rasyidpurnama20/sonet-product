# Batch 1 — Lagu 01–05 (Core/UI & Naratif)

Game: **Garuda Emblem** (Team Batalion) — 2D comical beat 'em up, kampus UNION.

---

## 01. Garuda Emblem — Main Theme
*(Title screen / tema utama)*

- **Story/Mood:** Heroik, jenaka, penuh semangat perlawanan mahasiswa. Pembuka yang langsung memompa adrenalin arcade dan memperkenalkan identitas "Garuda Emblem".
- **Chord Progression:** Verse `Am – F – C – G` (i–VI–III–VII, heroic minor); Chorus/Hook naik ke `C – G – Am – F` (III–VII–i–VI).
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro (8 bar, riff garuda) → A (verse) → B (pre-hook) → Hook → Bridge (key-lift) → **Loop** kembali ke A.
- **Instrumen:** Distorted electric guitar (rhythm + lead), chiptune square-lead 8-bit, live drums, synth bass, brass stab aksen.
- **Referensi Genre:** *Streets of Rage 4*, *Scott Pilgrim vs. The World: The Game*, J-rock arcade / power-pop.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Calm** (intro menu, hanya gitar + pad), **Combat** (full distorsi + drum), **Victory stinger** 4 bar.

---

## 02. Pick Your Fighter — Character Select
*(Layar pemilihan karakter)*

- **Story/Mood:** Stylish, percaya diri, groovy — momen "pilih jagoanmu" sebelum chaos dimulai. Cocok untuk mode co-op (Farid & Rani).
- **Chord Progression:** `Dm7 – G7 – Cmaj7 – A7` (ii–V–I dengan turnaround A7), nuansa funk/acid-jazz.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro 2 bar → vamp loop 8 bar (groove) → fill 1 bar saat kursor berpindah karakter.
- **Instrumen:** Slap bass, clavinet/Rhodes, wah-wah guitar, drum funk tight, horn hits.
- **Referensi Genre:** *Jet Set Radio*, char-select *Tekken/Street Fighter*, funk arcade.
- **Variasi Loop:** Layer melodi per karakter — **Farid** = guitar lead, **Rani** = synth lead; **lock-in stinger** saat karakter dikonfirmasi.

---

## 03. Newsroom HQ — Base Camp Garuda Emblem
*(Hub markas UKM jurnalistik)*

- **Story/Mood:** Hangat, santai, akrab. Markas pers mahasiswa tempat istirahat, mengatur strategi, dan ambil quest. Aman dari kekacauan luar.
- **Chord Progression:** `Cmaj7 – Am7 – Fmaj7 – G7` (I–vi–IV–V), lofi & mellow.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Loop 16 bar tanpa klimaks; section A (tema) ↔ B (variasi), ambient/no-build.
- **Instrumen:** Lofi piano/Rhodes, drum boom-bap halus, upright bass, vinyl crackle, gitar clean mellow.
- **Referensi Genre:** Lofi hip-hop, *Persona* daytime, hub theme *Yakuza*.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Siang** (cerah, lengkap) vs **Malam** (lebih sunyi, tambah pad + rain SFX); stinger pendek saat quest baru tersedia.

---

## 04. Campus Roam — Free-Roam Map
*(Eksplorasi area kampus / world map)*

- **Story/Mood:** Penasaran, eksploratif, ringan. Mengiringi pemain menjelajah kampus yang ramai sekaligus mencurigakan.
- **Chord Progression:** `G – D – Em – C` (I–V–vi–IV), upbeat & optimis.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Intro → A → B → **Loop**; ditata modular agar layer bisa ditambah/dikurangi per zona.
- **Instrumen:** Acoustic + electric guitar, marimba/ukulele, perkusi ringan, bass, whistle/whistling lead.
- **Referensi Genre:** Hub open-world indie, plaza *Splatoon*, adventure platformer.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Layer adaptif** — instrumen bertambah saat mendekati gerbang fakultas; versi **tegang** (drone tipis) saat area "panas".

---

## 05. Kantin Black Market — Shop / Upgrade
*(Toko item & upgrade tersembunyi di kantin)*

- **Story/Mood:** Misterius tapi jenaka — transaksi "gelap" di balik kantin kampus. Sneaky, cool, sedikit komikal.
- **Chord Progression:** `Em – Am – B7 – Em` (i–iv–V–i, harmonic minor) dengan sisipan jazzy `Cmaj7 – B7`.
- **Struktur Lagu:** Loop 8 bar berbasis walking bass; vamp santai, ruang untuk SFX transaksi.
- **Instrumen:** Muted jazz guitar, upright bass, brushed drums, vibraphone, finger snaps.
- **Referensi Genre:** Noir-jazz, *Cowboy Bebop*, shop/merchant theme bernuansa cool.
- **Variasi Loop:** **Buy stinger** (vibraphone arpeggio) saat membeli; versi **"harga naik / stok langka"** lebih tegang (tambah minor 2nd).
