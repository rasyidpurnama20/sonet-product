## 🎨 Visual Design Philosophy V3

### Core Principle: **MAXIMUM CLARITY AT ALL COSTS**

1. **Size Matters**: If in doubt, make it BIGGER
2. **Stroke Everything**: All text has thick stroke outline
3. **Bold Everything**: No thin or light fonts
4. **Contrast First**: Pure white vs pure dark, no in-betweens
5. **Simple Shapes**: No subtle gradients, clear solid colors
6. **Thick Borders**: Everything has 15-30px borders
7. **Generous Spacing**: No cramped layouts
